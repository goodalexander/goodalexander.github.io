    Level, week and maintenance

    How it decides

    1. Sleep or a falling lift: train at 2 sets for a week. Food stays the same.
    2. Hungry or worn out while on the menu, sleeping enough and still losing: go up one level now.
    3. 7-day average reaches 166 lb: add about 500 kcal a day for each 1 lb a week you were losing, and move to the closest level (the calculator does the math). After that, losing or gaining more than 0.5 lb a week moves you one level; otherwise stay. Keep between 163 and 168 lb.
    4. Otherwise no change before week 4, within two weeks of the last change, or with fewer than 6 days on the menu.
    5. Losing more than 1.5 lb a week: up one level. 1–1.5 lb: keep going. 0.5–1 lb: keep going if your waist is down. Slower: down one level.

      Why it worksThe reasoning and the studies
      • 2,000 kcal. Estimated burn is 2,500–2,800 kcal a day with this activity, so you lose about 1 lb a week.
      • 199 g protein. About 1.1 g per pound. On average, extra protein above about 0.7 g per pound added little more muscle [2]; higher intakes are recommended while dieting [3].
      • Eating 09:30–18:00. It worked for you before and keeps dinner with the family. An 8-hour window kept strength while cutting fat in trained men [1]; eating earlier improved insulin sensitivity and blood pressure in a small trial [7].
      • Lifting at 12:30. You train fed, three hours after breakfast, in the quietest hour of the trading day.
      • Easy cardio daily. Builds fitness at little recovery cost. In a large observational study, fitter adults had lower long-term mortality [5].
      • 8 hours of sleep. In a small two-week controlled study, dieting on 5.5 hours of sleep cut fat loss by 55% [4].
      • Creatine. The most-studied safe supplement for strength [6]. It adds 1–3 lb of water early, which is why the first food change waits until week 4.

      Get a physical with blood work in the first two weeks and again around week 12. Stop and get checked for chest pain, fainting or unusual breathlessness.
