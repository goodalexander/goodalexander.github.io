      US markets run 10:30–17:00 Puerto Rico time from Nov 1, 2026 to Mar 13, 2027 and 09:30–16:00 from Mar 14. Puerto Rico has no daylight saving, so only the market rows move. Lifting sits in the midday lull in both seasons: after the 08:30 and 10:00 New York data releases, before the 14:00 Fed announcements, and clear of earnings, which print before the open or after the close.

      02 / The six rules

      Everything else follows from these

      1. Eat between 09:30 and 18:00. Outside it: water, black coffee, electrolytes.
      2. Eat the same three meals daily. Breakfast 09:30, lunch 13:30 with the whey shake right after lifting, dinner 17:30 with family.
      3. Lift 12:30–13:20 in the midday market lull, three hours after breakfast. Monday, Wednesday and Friday in weeks 1–3, then Monday–Friday once you have earned it.
      4. Easy cardio every morning at 06:15, before breakfast. 30 minutes on weekdays, longer on weekends.
      5. Lights out 22:00, wake 06:00, weekends included.
      6. Sunday: at most one calorie change, following the check-in steps in order.

      04 / Training

      The trainer's split, earned in three steps

      10–12 reps (squats 12–15), stopping 1–2 reps short of failure. Two light warm-up sets on the first lift. Rest 2 minutes on the first two exercises, 60–90 seconds on the rest. When every set reaches the top of its range, add 5 lb (upper body) or 10 lb (legs) next session.

      Ready for the next step?

      Move up only when all four hold. Otherwise stay at your current step and recheck next Sunday.

      06 / Sunday check-in

      One decision a week

      Weigh daily after waking and keep a 7-day average; measure your waist at the navel on Sundays. Enter the numbers and the five steps run in order. Sleep and training fixes can stack; food changes at most once.

      The calorie ladder
      LevelAdds to the level belowkcal/dayProtein

      The top covers the estimated maintenance range (2,600–2,800 kcal) with room to spare. If a step would move you above +6 or below −1, stay where you are and see a doctor or dietitian. "Full menu" means your current level, including the allowed swaps, with nothing unplanned added or left out.

      The five steps, written out
      1. Recovery fixes (any week; training and sleep only). Under 7 hours of sleep on 3+ nights: 2 sets this week and fix the sleep routine. First lift down two sessions in a row with sleep on track: 2 sets for one week. Either sign still there after two weeks: see your doctor.
      2. Under-fueling (any week; overrides every waiting period). Lifts still down after a 2-set week, or hunger or fatigue disrupting work on 4+ days, while on the menu, sleeping 7+ hours and still losing: up one level, stop.
      3. Hold entry (first Sunday at or below 166 lb). The remaining deficit is about 500 kcal a day per 1 lb a week of loss. Add it to your level's kcal, move to the closest level, stop. Expect a 1–3 lb rebound over two weeks, mostly glycogen and water.
      4. Wait. No change before week 4, within two weeks of the last change, or with fewer than 6 of 7 days on the menu.
      5. Weight action. Cutting, by weekly loss: more than 1.5 lb, up one level; 1.0–1.5 lb, stay; 0.5 to under 1.0 lb, stay if the waist is down, otherwise down one; under 0.5 lb or gaining, down one. Hold, inside 163–168 lb: falling more than 0.5 lb a week, up one; within ±0.5 lb, stay; rising more than 0.5 lb, down one. Above the band and falling, or below it and rising: stay. Below the band and flat or falling: up one. Above the band and flat or rising two checks in a row: down one.

      Maintenance is confirmed after two "maintenance level" results in a row. Then keep weighing daily, run the Hold check on the first Sunday of each month, and go back to weekly checks after any other result.

        09 / Why it works

        The reasoning, with sources

        ~2,000 kcal to start

        Estimated maintenance at 188 lb with this activity is about 2,600–2,800 kcal, a deficit of roughly 600–800 kcal a day. From week 4 the ladder steers by your measured rate instead.

        200 g protein

        About 1.1 g per pound, above the ~0.7 g/lb (1.6 g/kg) intake beyond which extra protein added little muscle on average [2], and within the range recommended for lifters in a deficit [3].

        09:30–18:00 eating

        It matches what worked before and keeps dinner at the family table. An 8-hour window cut fat mass while keeping strength in resistance-trained men [1]; an early window improved insulin sensitivity and blood pressure in a small controlled trial [7].

        Lifting at 12:30

        You train fed, three hours after breakfast, with lunch straight after, in the quietest hour of the trading day in both seasons.

        Earned volume

        Three days at 2 sets, then five days at 2 sets, then 3 sets: about 18 sets a session at most, so you recover while eating less. The full 4-set program comes back at goal weight.

        Daily easy cardio

        Talking-pace cardio builds aerobic fitness at little recovery cost; weekly intervals raise VO₂max, and higher fitness is associated with lower long-term mortality [5].

        8 hours of sleep

        In a controlled study, dieting on 5.5 hours of sleep cut fat loss by 55% and increased loss of fat-free mass [4].

        Creatine

        5 g a day of creatine monohydrate supports strength and is the most-studied safe supplement [6]. Expect 1–3 lb of water weight when you start.

        Caveats

        Calories and macros are planning figures from package labels and USDA data; check your packages, which change by batch. The studies support the approach, not this exact window or schedule. Get a physical with blood work (typically lipids, A1c, kidney function) in the first two weeks and again around week 12. Stop exercising and get checked promptly for chest pain, fainting or unusual breathlessness.
